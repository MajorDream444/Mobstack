# Upstreams and Attribution

**Status:** PROPOSAL

MobStack is influenced by external systems, including Gstack's opinionated specialist roles, stage-based workflows, artifact handoffs, review gates, and test-before-ship discipline.

MobStack is not a wholesale fork of Gstack. Its venture lifecycle, evidence model, ownership gates, human-decision rule, MAIM relationship, HAMAL relationship, MOB architecture, and industry-learning contract are original requirements.

## Reuse rule

Before copying code, templates, commands, or substantial expression from another repository:

1. record the repository and exact revision;
2. inspect its license;
3. preserve required notices and attribution;
4. identify copied versus independently authored material;
5. prevent upstream terminology from silently becoming HAMAL doctrine;
6. test whether the dependency is necessary.

Patterns may inspire original implementation. Copied implementation retains its applicable license.
