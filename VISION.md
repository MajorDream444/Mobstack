# MobStack Vision v1.0

**Status:** PROPOSAL

## Goal

Build an open, portable execution system that helps people transform ideas into evidence-backed, locally owned ventures by coordinating specialist AI agents, human decision-makers, approved playbooks, and auditable handoffs.

MobStack is not merely a software-shipping assistant. It is infrastructure for venture formation, capability transfer, institutional memory, and earned ownership.

## Distinctive lifecycle

1. **Imagine** — expose the larger human and commercial possibility.
2. **Ground** — classify evidence, conflicts, assumptions, proposals, and unknowns.
3. **Architect** — define entities, authority, incentives, ownership, risks, and data rights.
4. **Build** — create approved artifacts, software, field systems, and operating cells.
5. **Verify** — test claims, usability, security, compliance, and readiness.
6. **Operate** — run controlled pilots and collect real evidence.
7. **Own** — convert verified contribution into legally documented capability or ownership.
8. **Multiply** — adapt proven patterns without copying unverified assumptions.

## End state

A founder or operator should enter with a problem, receive structured guidance, work with an accountable MOB, preserve every important source and decision, and leave with portable knowledge, evidence, systems, and a clearer path to ownership.

MobStack succeeds when the intelligence survives the chat, the system survives the agent, and the local builder can eventually operate without the external helper.
